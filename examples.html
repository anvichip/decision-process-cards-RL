Three claims, three decision processes

01

DPO

Direct Preference Optimization

A response policy fit to fixed pairwise preferences, derived from a KL-regularized preference-RL objective.

Claim tier
Fixed-data preference optimization derived from a KL-regularized preference-RL objective.
State or belief
Prompt or dialogue context; no external environment state or learned belief.
Action granularity and horizon
Whole responses or response pairs; terminal comparison, no within-episode discount.
Reward and cost
Pairwise preferences imply a log-ratio reward; report any judge used for evaluation.
Prompt/task support
Prompts drawn from the preference dataset and the paper's reported eval sets.
Response/trajectory support
Support comes from logged chosen/rejected responses and base-policy likelihood.
Value and uncertainty
No learned critic; uncertainty enters only via preference noise and judge disagreement.
Model interface
No transition, observation, reward, or cost model for an external environment.
Adaptation surface
Persistent weight update only; no memory write or tool-state change claimed.

Claim ceiling: supports fixed-data preference alignment — not exploration, online control, or safe deployment adaptation.

02

PPO-RLHF

Response-level RLHF via PPO

A policy optimized against a learned reward model with reference-policy KL regularization.

Claim tier
Reward-driven policy optimization at the response-level RLHF stage.
State or belief
Prompt plus generated prefix, treated as one terminal decision under stated conditions.
Action granularity and horizon
Token generation collapsed to a whole-response action; reward is terminal, not token-wise.
Reward and cost
Learned reward model plus fixed-reference KL; KL and PPO clipping play different roles.
Prompt/task support
Training prompt distribution; report task and user coverage of that set.
Response/trajectory support
Policy-sampled completions and base-policy reachability, not exhaustive prompt coverage.
Value and uncertainty
PPO critic estimates return; reward-model uncertainty is a separate, often unreported, question.
Model interface
Known token concatenation only; no external transition model required.
Adaptation surface
Policy updated between episodes; a changing reward model needs its own versioning report.

Claim ceiling: supports response-level reward-driven alignment — not long-horizon control or a safety guarantee from KL alone.

03

GRPO

Verifier-reward optimization for math

Group-relative policy-gradient updates from verifier rewards over sampled solutions.

Claim tier
RL with verifiable rewards — group-relative updates from a programmatic verifier.
State or belief
Math prompt and generated solution prefix; no external agent state.
Action granularity and horizon
Groups of sampled solutions per prompt; group-normalized advantage, not token-level credit.
Reward and cost
Verifier reward, group-relative advantage, reference-KL regularization; watch verifier false positives.
Prompt/task support
Covered math prompt distribution; transfer beyond it is unverified.
Response/trajectory support
Base-policy pass@k / reachability sets the real support region, not the group size.
Value and uncertainty
Group baseline replaces a critic — it doesn't remove calibration or credit-assignment questions.
Model interface
Verifier interface only; no learned environment or world model.
Adaptation surface
Policy weights updated between training batches; no deployment-time adaptation claimed.

Claim ceiling: supports verifier-reward improvement on covered math tasks — not broad reasoning or agentic competence under a shifted verifier.
